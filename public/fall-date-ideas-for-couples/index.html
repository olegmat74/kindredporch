30 Fall Date Ideas for Couples (Cheap, Cozy and Outdoorsy)

Fun fall date ideas: pumpkin patches, scenic drives, apple picking, haunted tours and cozy nights in.

Fall in the desert comes late, but when it hits, we go out every weekend. Here are thirty ideas, mixed between free, cheap and splurge.

The idea that finally made this click for me came from James Bauer's free presentation on the hero instinct.

Outdoors

  1. Pumpkin patch and corn maze.
  2. Apple or pear picking.
  3. Scenic drive with a thermos of cider.
  4. Sunset hike and picnic.
  5. Fall farmers market brunch.
  6. Bonfire with friends.
  7. Leaf-peeping road trip.
  8. Outdoor movie night.
  9. Hayride.
  10. Stargazing on a clear cold night.

Make the season feel like the beginning again

Fall dates are lovely. Pair them with what actually builds attraction and they stick. Here's the guide I used.

Out on the town

  1. Haunted house or ghost tour.
  2. Oktoberfest-style beer garden.
  3. Wine or cider tasting.
  4. Fall festival or craft fair.
  5. Cozy bookstore and coffee.
  6. Pottery class.
  7. High school football game.
  8. Local theater play.
  9. Thrift-store challenge: $10 each for the other's outfit.
  10. Try a new soup place every week.

Cozy at home

  1. Carve pumpkins.
  2. Bake a pie from scratch.
  3. Horror movie marathon.
  4. Make caramel apples.
  5. Puzzle and cocoa.
  6. Write each other a letter to open next fall.
  7. Plan Thanksgiving together.
  8. Rake leaves, then jump in them.
  9. Candle-making kit.
  10. Read the same book and talk about it.
