About

I'm Marisol, a former middle-school counselor's aide living in Tucson with my husband, two kids and a very loud cat. I started Kindred Porch to write down the small things that kept our marriage warm through new babies, job changes and one very long kitchen remodel.

I'm not a therapist. These are notes from real life, plus what I've learned from books and courses along the way.

— Marisol

Kindred Porch is one woman's notes on long-term love. It isn't therapy or counseling. If you feel unsafe, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233.
