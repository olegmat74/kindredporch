40 Sweet Texts to Send Him (Good Morning, Flirty and Just Because)

Cute texts to send your boyfriend or husband: good morning messages, flirty texts, support texts and appreciation notes.

A text takes six seconds and can change his whole afternoon. Steal these, change the words to sound like you.

Want to understand what he isn't saying out loud? Watch the free His Secret Obsession video before you spend anything.

Good morning

  1. Woke up thinking about you. Good start to a day.
  2. Coffee tastes better when you make it. Just saying.
  3. Go get 'em today. I believe in you.
  4. Morning, handsome.
  5. Already looking forward to tonight.
  6. Hope today is kind to you.
  7. You looked so peaceful sleeping. Didn't want to wake you.
  8. Big day? You've got this.

Flirty

  1. Can't stop thinking about last night.
  2. Wear the blue shirt tonight.
  3. I have a surprise for you later.
  4. You're very distracting, even from across town.
  5. Guess what I'm thinking about.
  6. Come home early?
  7. You still give me butterflies.
  8. Dinner's on me. Dessert too.

Support

  1. Proud of you for handling that.
  2. Whatever happens today, I'm on your side.
  3. You don't have to fix it tonight. Rest.
  4. Thank you for working so hard for us.
  5. I trust your judgment on this.
  6. Need anything? I'm here.
  7. You're stronger than this week.
  8. Call if you want to vent. No advice, just ears.

Notice how many of those say "I trust you" or "I'm proud of you." Those are the texts men tend to remember. I go into why in my honest His Secret Obsession review.

For more on why small words of respect land so hard with men, see what James Bauer teaches.

Just because

  1. Remember our first date? I do.
  2. Saw a dog that looked like you. Cute.
  3. Our song came on in the store.
  4. Thanks for fixing the sink. My hero.
  5. I like you. A lot.
  6. You make ordinary days good.
  7. Grocery list: milk, eggs, you.
  8. Lucky me.

Appreciation

  1. Thank you for always making me laugh.
  2. You're a great dad.
  3. I noticed you took care of that. Thank you.
  4. I'd choose you again.
  5. You make me feel safe.
  6. I love how you love our family.
  7. You're my favorite person to do nothing with.
  8. Home is wherever you are.
