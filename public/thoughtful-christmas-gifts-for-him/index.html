Thoughtful Christmas Gifts for Him That Aren't Socks

Christmas gift ideas for your boyfriend or husband: experiences, personal gifts, practical upgrades and gifts under $30.

Every December I stare at my husband's wish list (it says "nothing") and panic. Here's what's worked better than socks.

I borrowed a few exact phrases for this from a relationship coach's guide and rewrote them in my own voice.

Experiences

  • Tickets to see his team or a band he loved in high school.
  • A class: knife skills, woodworking, BBQ.
  • A night away within driving distance.
  • A tasting: hot sauce, whiskey, coffee.

Personal and sentimental

  • A letter listing twelve things you admire about him.
  • A photo book of your year.
  • An engraved pocket knife or watch.
  • His grandma's recipe, framed.
  • A star map of the night you met.

Practical upgrades

  • Good slippers or a heavy robe.
  • A quality flashlight or multi-tool.
  • A better wallet.
  • Wireless earbuds for the gym.
  • A cast iron skillet.

Under $30

  • His favorite snacks in a custom box.
  • Coupon book with real, specific offers.
  • A mug with an inside joke.
  • A puzzle of a photo of you two.

The gift underneath
