Relationship Journal Prompts for Couples (Printable-Friendly)

Journal prompts for couples to reflect, reconnect and plan: gratitude, memories, conflict and future dreams.

We started a shared notebook on the kitchen counter two years ago. Some weeks we both write, some weeks nobody does. It still became one of my favorite parts of us.

How to use these

One prompt each per week. Write separately, then read each other's answers on Sunday morning. No arguing about answers. Just "thank you for writing that."

Gratitude

  • Three things you did this week that I noticed.
  • A moment I felt lucky to be with you.
  • Something you do that makes my life easier.

Writing helps you see him differently

Memories

  • The first time I knew I loved you.
  • Our funniest disaster.
  • A trip I'd repeat exactly the same.

Understanding each other

  • When I feel most loved by you.
  • Something I wish you understood about my day.
  • When I feel most respected by you.
  • What I need when I'm stressed.

That "respected" prompt is the one my husband took longest to answer, and his answer surprised me. It's closely related to the idea I wrote about in my review of His Secret Obsession.

Conflict and repair

  • An argument I think we handled well.
  • One thing I want to do better next time.
  • A phrase that calms me down.

Future

  • One thing I want us to try this year.
  • Our home in ten years.
  • A tradition I want to start.
