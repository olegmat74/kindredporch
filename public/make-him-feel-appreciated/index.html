15 Ways to Make Your Husband Feel Appreciated

Small, specific ways to show appreciation to your husband or boyfriend, from words to actions he'll actually notice.

Most husbands I know aren't asking for grand gestures. They want to be noticed for what they already do. Here are fifteen ways, none expensive.

  1. Thank him for invisible work. Taking out the trash, checking the tire pressure, handling the car insurance.
  2. Brag about him to others, where he can hear.
  3. Ask his advice on something you actually value his opinion about.
  4. Let him finish the story without correcting the details.
  5. Leave a note in his truck or laptop bag.
  6. Make his favorite meal on a random Wednesday.
  7. Protect his downtime. Give him an hour alone with no guilt.
  8. Say "you were right." It costs nothing.
  9. Notice effort, not just results.
  10. Hug him first when he gets home.
  11. Text him a specific compliment (40 ready-made texts here).
  12. Back him up in public, disagree in private.
  13. Show interest in his hobby, even the boring one.
  14. Plan a date around what he loves.
  15. Tell him why you chose him.
